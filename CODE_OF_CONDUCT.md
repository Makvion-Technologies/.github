# Code of Conduct

Makvion Technologies is a professional software studio. Everyone who contributes to our repositories — employees, contractors and collaborators — is expected to treat others with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be clear, direct and professional in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before escalating.
- Protect client and company information. Never share secrets, credentials or private customer data in public channels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or any behaviour that makes it hard for others to do their work has no place here.

## Reporting

If something crosses a line, email [contact@makvion.com](mailto:contact@makvion.com). Reports are handled privately by the company.

We may remove content, restrict access, or take other action we judge necessary to keep the workplace professional.
